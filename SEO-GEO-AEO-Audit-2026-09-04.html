C0 — Production serves an empty application shell on every URL

This is the finding that explains "everybody thinks we do plaster only."

Two independent non-JS fetches of the live site returned:

URL fetchedTitle returnedBody content
/"Venetian Plaster Houston — Architectural Finishes & Painting | South Coast"none
/servicesthe same home-page title"appears to be an empty application shell with no visible body text"

Neither fetch found the words wood staining, cabinet painting or interior painting anywhere. No H1. No H2s. No body copy.

That is precisely what GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and Applebot-Extended see today: one plaster-titled empty page. Google renders JavaScript and copes; the AI engines largely do not. No amount of on-page copy fixes this while the site ships as a client-rendered SPA.

Fix: already built — the prerender step produces 32 real HTML files with correct titles, meta, canonicals and JSON-LD. It only needs deploying.

Impact: AI citation rate (currently structurally impossible) · indexable pages 5 → 32. Owner: Dev.

C1 — sameAs is an empty array

index.html ships "sameAs": []. Generative engines use sameAs to resolve that your site, Google Business Profile, Facebook page and Yelp listing are one entity. Without it, nothing connects your site to your 18 reviews.

Fix: populate with GBP, Instagram, Facebook and Yelp. Facebook and Yelp URLs located during research; GBP and Instagram still needed.

Impact: AI citation rate · entity confidence. Owner: Dev + client.

CRITICAL
LOCALGEO

C2 — NAP conflict between your schema and third-party listings

Site schema says addressLocality: "Houston" with no street address, and geo coordinates 29.7604 / −95.3698 — downtown Houston, not your premises. Yelp and Manta both list 63 Waterford Pointe Cir, Sugar Land, TX 77479.

Inconsistent NAP is the most common reason a local business misses the map pack, and it breaks entity resolution for AI engines.

Fix: choose the canonical address; make site schema, GBP, Yelp, Facebook and BBB agree exactly. A service-area business hides the address on GBP while the site still carries the registered one.

Impact: local pack inclusion. Owner: Client + dev.

SEO

H1 — Area pages are 84% shared template

Rendered area pages average 794 words, but only ~129 words differ between any two. The other ~665 — service card grid, quote form, reviews, nearby areas, CTAs — is identical across all 21.

They are not thin. They are near-duplicates, which is the actual doorway-page risk signal. What protects them today is that the unique 129 words are genuinely specific: real neighborhood names and real local conditions (shade-driven rot in Memorial, sun-fade in Katy).

Fix: raise unique content to ~350 words — add 3 area-specific FAQs (also earns FAQPage schema, H2) plus one local proof element: a completed project, a street-level detail, or a geo-alt-tagged photo.

Impact: ranking position for "[service] + [neighborhood]". Owner: Dev + client.

AEO

H2 — Area pages have no FAQPage schema

Service pages carry it; the 21 area pages do not. The crawl confirms the only question-shaped heading on an area page is "What do you need done?" — the embedded quote form, not answerable content. Area pages are exactly the queries that trigger AI Overviews and People Also Ask.

Fix: add faqs to AreaDef; emit FAQPage in AreaPage.tsx and prerender.mjs. Owner: Dev.
